# Deviation and scope log

This log separates the frozen estimands from constraints discovered during execution. It is part of the reproducibility bundle and prevents the final report from silently presenting an unavailable control as completed.

## 1 October 2026 - dataset and prompt freeze

- The supplied file has 2,000 claim rows but 1,972 multilingual dependency groups. The split contains 1,200/400/400 rows and 1,172/400/400 groups. Two mixed-label training groups are excluded from group-level probe fits. This unit correction was made before confirmatory dataset-v2 analysis.
- No documented topic labels exist. The allocation matches label, pair-normalized length quartile, negation, number presence, and automatic translation-risk status. It does not claim topic matching.
- Prompt configuration SHA-256 is `496c64768e1c52cfd224733d5fc49721d5ab681839394f179ac0d5cc5cafcf1b`. The rendered activation and behavioral prompt hashes are stored in `data/prompts_v1/manifest.json`.
- Translated prompts and dataset corrections were researcher-screened but were not independently reviewed by bilingual speakers. Runs proceeded so that computational analyses could be completed; language-specific conclusions remain provisional, and automatic-risk exclusion is only a sensitivity analysis rather than a substitute for human validation.
- Full continuation sequence log likelihood, summed over answer tokens, is the primary behavioral margin. Per-token-normalized scores were saved for sensitivity because translated answers have unequal token lengths.

## 1 October 2026 - model access and requested scope

- Exact public revisions were frozen in `config/model_revisions.json`. Official Llama-3.1-8B is manually gated, and the remote host had no authenticated Hugging Face token. It is therefore an unavailable planned replication, not a negative result.
- Qwen3-8B-Base, Apertus-8B-2509, and Gemma-7B form the executable model set. Gemma is the direct comparison to Li et al.; Qwen and Apertus are the cross-family primary models available without gated credentials.
- The user requested mass-mean probes only. Logistic hidden-state probes are omitted even though the initial design listed them as a sensitivity; scalar logistic mappings in RQ-C remain probability calibrators and are not representation probes.
- Human translation annotation and Cohen's kappa cannot be fabricated. The bilingual review pack is delivered for later annotation, and the report marks this requirement incomplete.

## Outcome-triggered observations retained without redefining endpoints

- Gemma's base-model behavioral judgments are strongly biased toward the localized true answer in several languages, producing label-imbalanced or single-class RQ-D quadrants. The frozen known/unknown rule is retained. Undefined AUROCs stay undefined; no threshold is tuned after seeing the result.
- Allocation percentiles and dependency-group bootstrap intervals are labeled distinctly. Repeated-allocation ranges are not described as confidence intervals.


## 2 October 2026 - X3 MMMLU context-limit compaction (frozen before any MMMLU outcome)

- Trigger: Qwen X3 extraction stopped with `ValueError: At least one example exceeds frozen context limit` (`runners/extract_selected_layer.py`, frozen limit `min(512, max_position_embeddings)` tokens including special tokens). No MMMLU activation, score, or performance value existed or was inspected when this rule was frozen; the diagnosis used only token lengths and the label-symmetric per-question maximum over both candidates.
- Diagnosis (exact frozen tokenizers; records / questions over 512 tokens of 1,140 / 570 per language). Qwen3-8B-Base: en 0/0, de 18/9, ar 18/9, hi 122/64, fr 15/8, es 8/4; max 1,945 tokens (hi). Apertus-8B-2509: en 0/0, de 5/3, ar 2/1, hi 27/14, fr 4/2, es 2/1; max 752 (hi). Mistral-7B-v0.3: en 0/0, de 24/12, ar 105/53, hi 144/74, fr 23/12, es 21/11; max 2,177 (hi). Overflow is concentrated in long-passage history items and professional law/medicine, and is driven by tokenizer fertility in Hindi and, for Mistral, Arabic. Gemma is not evaluated on MMMLU (checkpoint unavailable).
- Frozen rule (`runners/compact_mmmlu_context.py`): for each question, language, and model tokenizer, if either candidate prompt exceeds the frozen limit, remove whole whitespace-delimited words from the start of the shared question text and prepend `… `, keeping the longest question suffix for which both candidate prompts fit. The same truncated question is used for the correct and incorrect candidate; answers, templates, prefixes, labels, IDs, subjects, and resampling groups are unchanged. Questions that already fit are byte-identical to `data/mmmlu_external_v1`. The rule uses no labels or scores and no language-specific resources; it is applied identically to every model and language. Rationale for keeping the suffix: in long MMLU items the passage precedes the interrogative stem, so the stem is retained.
- All 570 questions are retained. The primary X3 estimate uses all compacted questions. Because compaction is unequal across languages (it mainly affects Hindi), a prespecified sensitivity reports the same metrics restricted to questions not compacted in any of the six languages for that model. Compacted files are written per model to `data/mmmlu_external_v1_fit512/<model_id>/` with a manifest listing every affected group, and caches go to `results/<model>/extensions/mmmlu_cache_fit512/`; the failed uncompacted Qwen cache attempt is retained as a failure record.
- Addendum, same day and still before any MMMLU activation or outcome existed: for one question (`mmmlu-13223`, security_studies) a single answer option alone exceeds the limit in Hindi (Qwen, Mistral) and Arabic (Mistral), so no question suffix can fit. Truncating that one answer would treat the two candidates differently. The frozen fallback is: if no suffix of at least one question word lets both candidates fit in any language, the question is excluded for that model in all six languages, so the evaluated question set stays parallel across languages. The decision uses only the label-symmetric maximum length. Realized effect (generated with the frozen-revision tokenizers): Qwen excludes `mmmlu-13223` (569 questions); Mistral excludes `mmmlu-13223` and `mmmlu-13305` (568; the latter has a 541-token Hindi answer prompt); Apertus excludes none (570). Questions compacted in at least one language: Qwen 63, Apertus 14, Mistral 73. Minimum retained question-word fraction: Qwen hi 0.16, Mistral hi 0.12 and ar 0.24, all other cells at least 0.59. Excluded and compacted IDs are listed in each per-model manifest.

## 2 October 2026 - outcome-triggered diagnostic: Apertus steering scale (no endpoint changed)

- Observed after the Apertus X2 outcome: neutral-continuation damage reached roughly 2 to 14 nats for most pairs but stayed near zero for EN→FR. Diagnosis from the frozen steering manifests: every Apertus unit mass-mean direction places weight about 0.78 on a single hidden dimension (index 1806), versus a maximum of about 0.10 to 0.16 in Qwen and Gemma. The frozen scaling (alpha × target-validation projection SD) therefore gives SDs of about 2,300 to 3,500 for five pairs and 22 for EN→FR, so one alpha unit is not comparable across Apertus pairs. Named Apertus directions (difficulty, language identity) also have |cosine| up to 0.95 with the truth direction.
- The frozen X2 rule and the core RQ-E analysis are kept unchanged. The report states that Apertus steering magnitudes are dominated by an outlier activation dimension and are not comparable across pairs or with other models. No outlier-dimension removal or rescaling is applied after the fact.
